I am moving the center of gravity here.

Not Facebook first. Not X first. Not TikTok first. Not some platform where the rules change after the post starts getting attention.

Here.

RealRyanNichols.com is my own front porch. It is the place I own, the feed I control, and the public record I can keep building without asking permission from an algorithm.

That does not mean I will never share links anywhere else. It means the full post, the receipts, the updates, the corrections, the video, the tip line, and the next move need to live here first.

## What changes now

The goal is simple: make this site move like a real newsroom without turning it into corporate news.

I want posts going up daily. Some will be short. Some will be long. Some will be videos. Some will be records-heavy investigations. Some will be personal updates from a father trying to rebuild his life after years of pressure, court fights, jail, family damage, and starting over.

The difference is that everything needs to point back to a page I own.

If I post about a January 6 case, it should connect back to the [J6 case section](/case).

If I post a video, it should live in the [video feed](/videos), not only inside somebody else's app.

If somebody has documents, screenshots, names, dates, or firsthand information, they should send it through the [tip line](/submit).

If the work helps people and they want to keep it going, they can [support the rebuild](/support).

## What I am going to post

I am building around several lanes:

- **Receipts and records.** Screenshots, filings, dates, transcripts, public records, and the paper trail.
- **January 6 case work.** Defendant profiles, timelines, documents, contradictions, and claims that need to be organized.
- **East Texas investigations.** Local issues, government records, law enforcement questions, family concerns, and stories that need daylight.
- **Personal rebuild updates.** Faith, family, work, recovery, discipline, and what life looks like after the system gets done with you.
- **Videos.** Short updates, longer explanations, live commentary, and clips that belong on my domain first.

The style is going to stay direct.

Short paragraphs.

Clear titles.

Dates when I have dates.

Names when they are public, relevant, and necessary.

Allegations marked as allegations.

Records separated from opinion.

Questions separated from conclusions.

## What makes a post worth publishing

Attention is not enough by itself.

A post has to do something.

It should show a record, explain a timeline, ask a precise question, point readers to a document, call for tips, correct a false claim, preserve a video, or move a case file forward.

If a post is only rage, it probably does not belong here.

If a post can help someone understand the record faster, it belongs here.

## How I want people to use this feed

Read it.

Share the link.

Comment on the site when comments are open.

Send documents instead of rumors.

Send dates instead of vague stories.

Send screenshots with context.

Send names only when you can explain why the name matters and what record supports it.

I am not trying to build a place where people pile on. I am trying to build a place where the record gets clearer.

## The daily rhythm

I am building toward three to five serious posts a day when the record supports it.

That does not mean three to five reckless posts. It means a steady rhythm:

- one post that explains what happened,
- one post that shows a record,
- one post that asks for missing information,
- one post that links readers deeper into the case,
- and, when needed, one post that corrects or updates the record.

That is how attention becomes useful.

Not noise.

Not drama.

A record people can follow.

## The bottom line

I spent too many years posting on land I did not own.

This is the home base now.

The feed, the videos, the case work, the tip line, the comments, the corrections, the support links, and the record all need to live here.

If you want the full story, come here.

If you have facts, send them here.

If you want to support the work, support it here.

No algorithm gets to decide whether the record matters.
