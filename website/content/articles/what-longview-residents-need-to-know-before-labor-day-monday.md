Monday will not be a normal service day in Longview.

The City of Longview says municipal offices and Longview Transit will be closed for Labor Day on September 7, 2026. The Longview Public Library is also closed through Monday, including its book drop and curbside service.

At the same time, a public Labor Day celebration is listed for late morning at Teague Park.

Here is the practical version of what residents should know before leaving home.

## City offices and buses are closed Monday

The city's [September 3 public notice](https://www.longviewtexas.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=6539) says City of Longview offices will close Monday in observance of Labor Day.

Longview Transit will also be closed Monday. The notice says bus service resumes Tuesday, September 8.

That matters most for people who depend on public transportation for work, groceries, appointments, or connections to other services. If Monday travel is necessary, make a plan today. Confirm a ride, reschedule a nonurgent trip, or check the destination's own holiday hours before arranging transportation.

The city notice confirms the transit closure and Tuesday restart. It does not promise that every other public or private transportation provider will follow the same schedule. Verify those separately.

## The library and its book drop are unavailable

The Longview Public Library is closed from Saturday, September 5, through Monday, September 7, according to the city's [Labor Day calendar listing](https://www.longviewtexas.gov/calendar.aspx?day=7&month=9&view=list&year=2026).

The closure includes the library book drop. Curbside pickup is unavailable as well. Library services are scheduled to resume Tuesday, September 8.

If you have materials due, do not leave them outside a closed drop. Use the library's official account tools to check your items, then return them when the facility and drop reopen. If a deadline or account question remains, contact the library after service resumes.

This is a small detail until someone drives across town with a bag of books and nowhere approved to leave them.

## A community celebration is scheduled at Teague Park

The city calendar lists a [Labor Day Celebration](https://www.longviewtexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=10942&calType=0&day=3&month=9&year=2026) for Monday from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Teague Park, 415 American Legion Boulevard.

The listing says the event is organized by the Gregg County Democratic Party. Its presence on the city calendar does not make it a city-run event, and residents should understand that distinction.

People considering attendance should check the event listing again before leaving. Weather, organizer decisions, parking instructions, or other details can change. The official city calendar confirms the listed time, place, and organizer as of verification early Sunday, September 6.

## What is known and what is not

The official notices establish four clear facts:

- City offices are closed Monday.
- Longview Transit is closed Monday and scheduled to return Tuesday.
- The library, book drop, and curbside pickup are unavailable through Monday and scheduled to return Tuesday.
- The community celebration is listed from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Teague Park.

The notices do not provide a universal schedule for county offices, courts, banks, trash collection, pharmacies, clinics, or private businesses. Do not assume one closure answers every question.

If a service matters to your Monday, verify it directly.

## A five-minute Sunday checklist

Before the holiday begins:

1. Check whether you planned to use Longview Transit Monday.
2. Move any library return or curbside pickup to Tuesday or later.
3. Confirm the hours of any other office or business you need.
4. Save the address and latest listing if you plan to attend the Teague Park event.
5. Share the transit or library closure with someone who may not see the city notice.

Public-service information is useful only when it reaches people before they waste a trip or miss a connection.
