A useful skill does not always need a semester, a subscription, or a long drive.

Sometimes it needs one Saturday afternoon at the public library.

The City of Longview’s official calendar lists a free Canva Basics class today, Saturday, September 19, from 1 to 4 p.m. in the PC Computer Lab at Longview Public Library, 222 W. Cotton Street.

Registration is required.

For a small-business owner, volunteer, job seeker, church team, teacher, or anybody tired of fighting with a blank flyer, this is practical public value hiding in plain sight.

## What the class covers

According to the [official City of Longview event listing](https://www.longviewtexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=10926), the class introduces Canva as a web-based publishing program.

The posted lesson includes:

- the kinds of publications and templates available in Canva
- editing text and adding text boxes
- adding images
- recoloring design elements
- basic graphic-design techniques
- creating a flyer from a built-in template

The event is scheduled for three hours, which gives students more room than a quick demonstration. The goal is not just to watch somebody click through the program. The listing says students will create a flyer.

That matters. A finished first project is easier to build on than a page of notes.

## Who can use this

The obvious audience is somebody who has never used Canva.

The real audience is larger.

A neighborhood group may need a clean event notice. A new business may need a service sheet before it can afford a designer. A job seeker may need to understand the kind of visual tool that appears in office and communications work. A nonprofit may need a volunteer to turn correct information into something people will actually read.

The library describes its changing monthly computer classes as support for career and personal development. That is a fair description of this one.

Knowing how to build one clear flyer will not make somebody a professional designer. It can remove one barrier between an idea and the people who need to see it.

## What you need before you go

The official listing names several prerequisites:

- Computer Basics
- Word Basics I and II
- a working email account

Internet Basics is highly recommended.

Those requirements suggest this is a beginner Canva class, not a first-ever computer class. If opening files, using a mouse, or working with email is still difficult, call the library and ask which class should come first.

The city also says signup is required. To register or confirm a seat, contact the library at **903-237-1351** or email the address listed on the event page at **BCHunter@longviewtexas.gov**.

## What is known and what is not

Here is what the city has published:

- **Date:** Saturday, September 19, 2026
- **Time:** 1 to 4 p.m.
- **Place:** Longview Public Library PC Computer Lab
- **Address:** 222 W. Cotton Street, Longview
- **Cost:** Free
- **Signup:** Required

The listing does not state how many seats remain, whether walk-ins will be accepted if space opens, or whether students need their own Canva account before arriving.

Do not assume a calendar listing means a seat is waiting. Call or email before making the trip.

## Why a free class like this matters

East Texas has plenty of people with good work and weak presentation.

That is not an insult. It is what happens when somebody knows how to clean a roof, run a food pantry, organize a fundraiser, or repair an engine but has never been shown how to turn the facts into a readable page.

Good design does not replace good work. It helps good work get understood.

A flyer needs a clear headline, the right details, enough contrast, and one obvious next step. Those same principles apply to a church announcement, a menu, a hiring notice, a social post, or a simple service sheet.

The software will change. The skill of arranging information so another person can act on it will keep paying.

## If today’s class is full

Do not write the opportunity off.

Ask the library about the next computer class and whether there is a waiting list. The [library’s computer classes page](https://www.longviewtexas.gov/3441/Computer-Classes) is the best place to watch for future sessions.

You can also use the call to ask which prerequisite class fits your current skill level. Starting one step earlier is still starting.

Longview residents already pay for public resources like this. Using them is not taking something you did not earn. It is what the resource is for.

## What to do next

Call **903-237-1351** now and ask whether a seat remains for Canva Basics at 1 p.m.

If there is room, bring the working email account the listing requires and arrive ready to build one flyer.

If the class is full, ask for the next date before you hang up.

One free afternoon will not build the whole business, campaign, ministry, or career.

It can build the first page people understand.

Who in Longview would benefit from knowing this class is available today?
