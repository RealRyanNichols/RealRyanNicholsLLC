If you want me to look at a story, send me records.

Not just outrage.

Not just "everybody knows."

Not just a screenshot with no date, no name, no link, and no way to check it.

Send the record.

That is how this site is going to stay useful.

## What I can actually use

The strongest tips usually have the same pieces:

- **Date.** When did it happen?
- **Location.** What city, county, agency, court, school, business, church, or office is involved?
- **Names.** Who is involved, and why does the name matter?
- **Documents.** Court filings, reports, letters, notices, orders, emails, records requests, or receipts.
- **Screenshots.** Full screenshots with the username, date, time, and surrounding context visible when possible.
- **Links.** Public posts, government pages, court records, news stories, archived pages, or videos.
- **Timeline.** What happened first, second, third, and what happened after that?
- **Status.** Is this active, resolved, under appeal, still waiting on records, or still developing?

If a story involves children, private family matters, sealed records, medical information, addresses, phone numbers, or other sensitive material, send it carefully. Do not blast private information into public comments.

Use the [tip line](/submit).

## What slows everything down

The hardest tips to work are the ones that arrive like this:

> "Look into this person. They are corrupt."

That is not enough.

I need the record that made you say that.

Who said what?

When?

Where?

What document proves it?

What screenshot shows it?

What agency has the record?

What case number, report number, filing number, or public link helps verify it?

If you do not have every answer, that is fine. Send what you have and clearly mark what you do not know.

Do not fill in blanks with guesses.

## How I read a tip

I am looking for the cleanest path to the truth.

First, I separate facts from allegations.

Then I look for dates.

Then I look for documents.

Then I look for contradictions.

Then I look for what is missing.

After that, I decide what kind of post fits the record:

- a short update,
- a full investigation,
- a timeline,
- a public-records question,
- a correction,
- a video explanation,
- or a case file entry.

Not every tip becomes a public post. Some tips need more records. Some tips are too private. Some tips are not ready. Some tips may be true, but still need documentation before I can responsibly write about them.

## What I will not do

I will not publish a serious accusation as fact just because somebody is angry.

I will not publish private information that does not need to be public.

I will not send readers to harass a person, a family, a witness, a public official, or a private citizen.

I will not pretend a rumor is a record.

I will not invent a court fact, a quote, a document, or a timeline because it would make the story hit harder.

If something is alleged, I will call it alleged.

If something is confirmed by a record, I will say what record supports it.

If something is missing, I will say what is missing.

That is how the work stays strong.

## What I need from January 6 defendants

For January 6 case work, the same rule applies.

Send documents.

Send docket numbers.

Send filings.

Send transcripts.

Send plea paperwork.

Send sentencing materials.

Send detention records.

Send screenshots only if they help explain the timeline.

Send the part of the story that the government, media, attorneys, or public record left out, but send it with something I can organize.

I am building this so people can understand the record faster.

## What I need from East Texas

For local investigations, send the paperwork first.

Police reports.

Body-camera request numbers.

Court filings.

County records.

School records where lawful.

Public meeting agendas.

Recorded statements.

Emails.

Texts.

Photos.

The point is not to stir up a mob. The point is to make the record clear enough that people can see what happened and what still needs to be answered.

## Send it the right way

If you have firsthand information, documents, screenshots, records, links, or a timeline, send it through the [tip line](/submit).

If the story is urgent because someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services first. This site is not 911.

If you want the story handled seriously, send it like a record.

Facts.

Dates.

Documents.

Links.

Names only when they matter.

That is how we turn attention into accountability.
