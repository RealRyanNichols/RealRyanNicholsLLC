Tonight I received a message I cannot ignore, and I'm doing with it what I always do with a threat: I'm putting it on the record.

Someone reached out to warn me. I am **not** going to tell you who, and I've removed their name and their photo, because they came to me to **help** me — not to hurt me — and I will not put a person who tried to protect me in harm's way. Here is what they said, word for word:

> "I have a friend who's in law enforcement, he is credible.. I don't generally like cops but this one he saved my life.. he's told me that there's members of law enforcement that want to hurt you.. like beat you up.. he didn't tell me who they are, but we can narrow it down to Harrison Marion County area.."

Let me be careful and clear about exactly what this is and what it is not.

This is a **warning, relayed to me.** A person I trust says someone *they* trust — who is in law enforcement — told them that there are members of law enforcement who want to physically hurt me. The words used were "beat you up." They could not name the individuals. What they could narrow it to was the **Harrison / Marion County area.**

I am **not** naming anyone. I don't know who they are, and I will not accuse a specific person of something I cannot prove. That is not what this is. This is me documenting a credible warning about a threat to my safety, from a source with law-enforcement contacts, and refusing to sit on it quietly.

They said "beat you up." Given everything that has already been done to me, and given where it reportedly comes from, I treat any of it as a threat to my safety and to my life. You do not get the benefit of the doubt when you are talking about putting hands on a man who has already been through what I have been through.

Here is why I take this seriously instead of brushing it off.

I have spent months documenting, in public, what has been done to me out of Harrison County — the [deadly-conduct charge where the gun never left the holster](/posts/deadly-conduct-gun-never-left-the-holster), the [weaponized lawfare](/posts/weaponized-lawfare-harrison-county), the [harassment I called the county itself to report](/posts/i-called-harrison-county-to-report-harassment). When you are already in the fight I'm in, and someone with a law-enforcement source tells you officers want to put their hands on you, and it points back to the exact county that has been coming after me, you do not get to pretend you didn't hear it.

So this is what I'm doing:

- **I'm putting it on the public record.** A threat that everyone can see is harder to act on than one made in the dark. If something happens to me, this is here — time-stamped, in my own words.
- **I'm preserving the evidence** — the message, who it came from, and when it arrived — and getting it in front of the people who need to see it, including my counsel.
- **I'm protecting the person who warned me.** Their name and face are redacted. Do not go looking for them. They did the right thing.

I'm not writing this for attention. I'm writing it because documentation is the only armor I have left. I have said for a long time that the most dangerous thing for the people who want to hurt me is a record they cannot bury. So here is the record.

And if you are one of the people this warning is about: know that it is written down now, in public, with my name on it.
