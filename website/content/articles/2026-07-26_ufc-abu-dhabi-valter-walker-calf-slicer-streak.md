*By the Real Ryan Nichols Editorial Team*

UFC Fight Night 282 — "UFC Abu Dhabi," headlined by Magomed Ankalaev's fifth-round TKO of Bogdan Guskov — landed on July 25, 2026, at Etihad Arena. The main event will get the highlight package. The prelim fight everyone kept reposting was a heavyweight bout that lasted less than two minutes.

Valter Walker submitted Thomas Petersen via calf slicer at 1:32 of round one. It was Walker's fifth consecutive win by submission, and MMA outlets are describing it as tying a modern-era finishing streak previously held solo by BJJ legend Demian Maia.

UFC Brasil posted the finish on X within minutes: [x.com/UFCBrasil/status/2081043477887066205](https://x.com/UFCBrasil/status/2081043477887066205) — "MAIS UMA FINALIZAÇÃO!!" ("ANOTHER FINISH!!"). UFC's own broadcast account followed with clip captions like "ANOTHER SUBMISSION FOR VALTER WALKER 🤯 Make that FIVE in a row for the heavyweight!" The clips have been reshared across fan accounts and MMA media all weekend.

## What actually happened in the cage

Walker is known for hunting heel hooks. This time he abandoned the usual approach and went to a calf slicer instead — a leg-compression submission that's far less common at the finish line than a heel hook or a rear-naked choke. Petersen tapped at 1:32.

That's the whole fight. Ninety-two seconds, one adjustment in technique, one tap.

[Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/brianmazique/2026/07/25/ufc-abu-dhabi-results-bonuses-and-highlights/), the [official UFC.com results page](https://www.ufc.com/news/ufc-abu-dhabi-results), [Bloody Elbow](https://bloodyelbow.com/2026/07/25/valter-walkers-historic-heel-hook-streak-ends-as-he-wins-in-92-seconds-at-ufc-abu-dhabi/) and [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/ufc-abu-dhabi-video-valter-160148976.html) all confirm the same sequence: fifth straight finish, new submission variety, immediate post-fight callouts. Walker used his microphone time to call out Alex "Poatan" Pereira and Ciryl Gane — a heavyweight and a light heavyweight, which tells you Walker isn't being shy about where he thinks he ranks right now.

## Why the "record" framing needs a caveat

Here's where I want to slow down instead of just riding the hype.

The "ties a modern-era streak record" line is coming from MMA media outlets doing their own comparison to Demian Maia's run, not from an official UFC record book entry. That distinction matters. It's very likely true in the sense these outlets mean it — five straight finishes is genuinely rare at this level — but "MMA outlets say" and "UFC officially confirms" are two different sentences, and I'm not going to blur them just because the clip is satisfying to watch.
