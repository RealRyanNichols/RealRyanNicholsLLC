## Public redaction rule

This public page does not publish private addresses, phone numbers, driver's-license data, cannabis-card material, medical records, minors' information, raw private messages that are not necessary to show publicly, or unredacted source files.

Those items can exist in the private evidence file, discovery file, subpoena file, or sealed/redacted court exhibit. They do not belong loose on the internet.

The public version shows what can be shown safely: redacted screenshots, filing previews, video receipts, public posts, public-records demand receipts, and source-chain notes.

## Classification key

- **FACT** - supported by an identified public record, public post, preserved screenshot, filing preview, video file, or official communication.
- **RYAN STATEMENT** - my personal account, preserved for testimony, declaration, or later corroboration.
- **DOCUMENTED INFERENCE** - a reasonable connection drawn from identified records, but still something that should be tested.
- **NEEDS AUTHENTICATION** - a screenshot, message, video, or file that should be authenticated through metadata, native files, platform records, custodian records, subpoena, sworn testimony, or hearing.
- **PRIVATE / NOT PUBLIC** - the item exists, but the public version is redacted or summarized because it contains sensitive personal information.

## What is intentionally not public

Some records are not posted here because public posting would create the wrong risk. That includes medical records, cannabis-card material, minors' information, unredacted addresses, private phone numbers, driver's-license information, and native files that should be authenticated before public release.

That does not mean they do not exist.

It means they belong in the private evidence file, discovery file, subpoena file, sealed exhibit, or redacted court filing.
