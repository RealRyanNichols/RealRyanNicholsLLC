The pitch is not "Ryan builds websites."

The pitch is:

> Stop renting attention. Make your website the home base, use social media as the billboard, and turn every post into a path back to your proof, your offer, your supporters, and your direct audience.

This keeps the page out of commodity web-design territory. The competition can sell a five-page website. Ryan sells an owned-feed strategy built from lived platform-enforcement experience, receipt-driven writing, public proof, and a path to money without ads.

## Common objections and answers

### "Why not just use Facebook, X, YouTube, or TikTok?"

Because those platforms are distribution, not home base. They can throttle, lock, bury, or delete the account. An owned site lets the creator keep the record, capture email/SMS, publish long-form proof, host the offer, and send every social click back to one place.

### "Isn't this just a website?"

No. A normal website is static. An owned feed is a public publishing system: posts, proof, video, screenshots, source links, calls to action, analytics, checkout, contact, and a reason to come back.

### "I already have followers. Why do I need this?"

Followers are rented attention unless you can reach them directly. The goal is to turn social followers into site visitors, subscribers, buyers, supporters, and people who know where the record lives.

### "Why is this worth $997?"

Comparable done-for-you site offers publicly cluster around $499 to $1,497, with premium personal-brand sites going higher. Ryan's offer is not the cheapest commodity site. It is a domain-first feed built around message, proof, posts, attention path, and monetization.

Sources:

- SiteMakert done-for-you packages, accessed 2026-05-31: https://sitemakert.com/done-for-you-packages
- BrandLauncher personal brand websites, accessed 2026-05-31: https://brand-launcher.com/
- CMMM Studios pricing, accessed 2026-05-31: https://cmmmstudios.com/pricing
- SwiftySites pricing, accessed 2026-05-31: https://swiftysites.com/home

### "Can I just use a cheap website builder?"

Yes, if all you need is a basic web presence. This offer is for people who need the site to carry a story, receipts, posts, offers, and audience capture. Cheap builders can publish pages. They do not automatically build the argument, the proof structure, the post rhythm, or the conversion path.

### "Will this make me money?"

Do not promise that. Say:

> The site gives attention somewhere to land. Money still comes from the offer, the proof, the trust, and the follow-through.

### "Can I take payments in crypto?"

Say:

> Card checkout comes first. Stablecoin checkout can be added through approved payment rails. Crypto here starts as payment infrastructure, not an investment scheme.

Source:

- Stripe stablecoin payment docs, accessed 2026-05-31: https://docs.stripe.com/payments/accept-stablecoin-payments?payment-ui=direct-api

### "Are you launching a coin?"

Answer:

> No tradable coin at launch. The responsible path is services first, payment rails second, prepaid service credits third, and any public token only after legal review. No investment promises.

- A place to publish posts, proof, videos, screenshots, links, and updates.

- Creators tired of algorithm dependency.

- Do not sell "uncensorable" as an absolute. Say "harder to erase from one platform."
