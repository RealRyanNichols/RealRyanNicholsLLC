## Meaningful additions and corrections

The profile now distinguishes the June 2021 complaint allegations, the facts Robert Morss adopted for a stipulated bench trial, the August 23, 2022 judicial findings, the May 24, 2023 sentence, the January 20, 2025 full pardon, and Morss's attributed post-release statements.

The prior profile did not clearly explain that Morss did not plead guilty. He waived a jury, adopted a stipulated statement of facts, and proceeded through a stipulated bench trial. The court found him guilty on three felony counts: robbery under 18 U.S.C. § 2111, assaulting/resisting/impeding officers with a dangerous weapon under 18 U.S.C. § 111, and obstruction under 18 U.S.C. § 1512(c)(2), with aiding-and-abetting provisions.

The profile now records the official sentencing terms: 66 months of imprisonment, 24 months of supervised release, and $2,000 restitution. The remaining counts were dismissed on the government's motion. A signed judgment is still needed before adding any financial term not stated in the official sentencing announcement.

The Office of the Pardon Attorney's public list contains the exact entry `MORSS, ROBERT`. The individual certificate PDF remains pending.
