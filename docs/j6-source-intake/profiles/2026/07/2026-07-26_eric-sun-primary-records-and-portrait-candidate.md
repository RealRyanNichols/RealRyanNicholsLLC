## Claims and characterization

### Established by the court record

- ECF 1 charged two Class B misdemeanors. At filing, those counts were allegations.
- ECF 9 is a signed agreement to plead guilty to both counts; it also records the potential penalties, contemplated restitution and assessments, and the parties' reserved sentencing positions.
- ECF 10 contains Sun's signed stipulated facts: LaGrange, Georgia residence; entry through the Senate Wing doors at about 3:02 p.m.; movement into the Rotunda while recording; departure at about 3:11 p.m.; Facebook photographs of himself inside; and later removal of those posts.
- ECF 13 continued release under specified conditions and set sentencing for May 8, 2025.
- ECF 14 is government advocacy requesting dismissal with prejudice after the January 20, 2025 proclamation.
- ECF 15 is the controlling judicial disposition: dismissal of the Information with prejudice, vacation of pending deadlines and hearings, and termination of the case. No sentence was imposed.

### Preserved conflict

ECF 14 calls the charging paper an “indictment.” ECF 1 is an Information, and ECF 15 expressly treats the motion's “indictment” reference as referring to the Information. The archive preserves the discrepancy and follows the court's clarification.
