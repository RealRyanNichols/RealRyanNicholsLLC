## Material correction

The prior profile contained only generic arrest-stage language and no case documents, events, docket number, judge, or disposition. The verified record does **not** support saying that Andrew Roman Carvajal pleaded guilty, was convicted, was sentenced, or received a conviction-based pardon.

ECF No. 35 states that Carvajal was arraigned on March 19, 2024 and pleaded not guilty to four misdemeanor counts. He later notified the court that he intended to accept a plea offer, but at the January 7, 2025 plea hearing he could not recall portions of the factual proffer relevant to the proposed offense. The court continued the hearing and did not accept a guilty plea.

On January 21, 2025, the government moved to dismiss the information with prejudice under Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 48(a), relying on the January 20 presidential proclamation's separate directive concerning pending January 6 prosecutions. Carvajal consented. On January 23, 2025, Judge Kollar-Kotelly dismissed the information with prejudice, vacated the continued plea hearing and release order, denied pending motions as moot, and closed the case. No conviction or sentence was entered.
