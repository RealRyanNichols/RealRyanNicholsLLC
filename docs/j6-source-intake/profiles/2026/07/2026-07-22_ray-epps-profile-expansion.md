This bundle replaces the prior short profile note, which repeated an unverified suspicion without providing the criminal case record, Epps's sworn interview, sentencing history, pardon, or later civil-court ruling. ## Verified chronology

- **January 21, 2022:** Epps gave a transcribed interview to the House Select Committee.
- **September 20, 2023:** He pleaded guilty in D.D.C. case `1:23-cr-00321-JEB` to one misdemeanor count under 18 U.S.C. § 1752(a)(2).
- **January 9, 2024:** Chief Judge James E. Boasberg imposed one year of probation, 100 hours of community service, $500 restitution, and a $25 assessment, with no prison term.
- **January 20, 2025:** The presidential proclamation granted a full, complete, and unconditional pardon to people convicted of covered January 6 offenses, including Epps.
- **May 8, 2026:** Judge Jennifer L. Hall dismissed Epps's amended defamation complaint against Fox News for failure to plausibly plead actual malice. The court did not hold that every challenged statement was true.
