## Meaningful addition

The archive added the June 30, 2021 memorandum opinion addressing press access to video clips associated with Federico Klein's April 9, 2021 detention hearing. Judge John D. Bates held that clips actually shown to the court were judicial records subject to the common-law presumption of public access. The court permitted recording, copying, downloading, retransmission, and publication of those clips. It denied access through that application to videos merely referenced in filings but never presented to the court.

This ruling concerned access to judicial records. It did not decide Klein's guilt, sentence, pardon status, or moral character.
