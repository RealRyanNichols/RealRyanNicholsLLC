## Verified record summary

Daniel Ray Caldwell was arrested on February 10, 2021 and detained pending trial after a published May 21, 2021 district-court opinion denied his request for release. The opinion expressly stated that its factual discussion was not a merits determination. Caldwell pleaded guilty on September 26, 2022 to assaulting, resisting, or impeding officers using a dangerous weapon. Judge Colleen Kollar-Kotelly sentenced him on February 1, 2023 to 68 months in prison, 36 months of supervised release, $2,000 restitution, and a $100 special assessment. President Donald J. Trump's January 20, 2025 proclamation granted a full pardon to covered convicted January 6 defendants who were not among the fourteen named commutation recipients; the Office of the Pardon Attorney's public certificate-recipient list includes `CALDWELL, DANIEL RAY`. A February 9, 2025 Tim Hale article documents a post-release interview in which Caldwell discussed his background, January 6, incarceration, and plans to seek release of Metropolitan Police Department video. Those interview claims remain attributed to Caldwell and the publisher pending native-media and transcript review.
