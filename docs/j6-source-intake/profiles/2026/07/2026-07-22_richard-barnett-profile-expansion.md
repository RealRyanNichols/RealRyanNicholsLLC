## Verified and attributed chronology

- **January 8, 2021 — Arrest.** The Justice Department announced Barnett’s arrest in Little Rock, Arkansas. The original complaint was an accusation, and the presumption of innocence applied at that stage.
- **January 29, 2021 — Indictment.** The district case is *United States v. Barnett*, No. 1:21-cr-00038-CRC-1, in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia.
- **January 23, 2023 — Trial verdict.** A jury found Barnett guilty on eight felony and misdemeanor counts. Descriptions of his conduct are attributed to the government’s evidence and the trial record rather than treated as judgments about his character.
- **May 24, 2023 — Sentence.** Judge Christopher R. Cooper imposed 54 months imprisonment, with concurrent shorter terms on misdemeanor counts, and 36 months supervised release.
- **June 9, 2023 — Direct appeal filed.** The D.C. Circuit opened *United States v. Richard Barnett*, No. 23-3086.
- **January 20, 2025 — Full pardon and release.** The presidential proclamation covered qualifying January 6 convictions. The Office of the Pardon Attorney’s certificate-recipient list includes `BARNETT, RICHARD`, and local reporting states Barnett was released from custody that night.
- **January 30, 2025 — Public statement.** In a Newsmax appearance, Barnett said he had “no regrets,” described imprisonment as having put him “through hell,” and explained his political interpretation of January 6. These are attributed statements, not archive findings.
- **February 2025 — Post-pardon vacatur, remand, and dismissal.** Reporting on the filings states that the government sought vacatur, the D.C. Circuit remanded, and the district court dismissed the case. The reported dismissal order stated that dismissal was not a judicial finding of innocence. Native orders remain pending capture.
- **September 29, 2025 — Financial-relief motion.** Barnett’s federal defenders reportedly requested return of $2,455 in restitution and special assessments. The report states the government did not oppose the request. No native order resolving it was located during this review.
