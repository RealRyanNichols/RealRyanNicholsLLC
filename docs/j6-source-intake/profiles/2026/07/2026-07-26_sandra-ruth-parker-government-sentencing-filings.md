## Verification boundary

ECF No. 1018 is prosecution sentencing advocacy, not a judicial ruling. The memorandum calculated a 97-to-121-month advisory-guidelines range for Sandra Parker, Laura Steele, Connie Meggs, and William Isaacs and argued for significant imprisonment. It stated that no fine should be imposed, requested three years of supervised release, and advocated special conditions.

Judge Mehta later sentenced Sandra Parker to 60 months of probation. The archive preserves the government’s position and the court’s sentence as separate procedural facts.

ECF No. 1021 states that footnote 9 of the sentencing memorandum was corrected and refers to a proposed corrected memorandum as Exhibit 1. The captured mirror file contains only the one-page notice. The corrected memorandum or exhibit was not present, so the archive does not infer the wording or effect of the correction.

The restitution section of ECF No. 1018 uses singular wording and refers to “Defendant Greene,” even though Michael Greene is not included in the five-defendant caption. The archive therefore does not assign that requested term to Sandra Parker without her signed judgment.
