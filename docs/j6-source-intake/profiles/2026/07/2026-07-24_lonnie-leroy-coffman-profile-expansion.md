## Verified procedural record

- January 6, 2021: later official records state that Coffman was taken into custody. The January 8 charging release instead described the arrest as occurring the previous day; the profile preserves this source conflict and distinguishes custody from the January 7 complaint proceedings.
- January 7, 2021: federal complaint and initial appearance.
- January 11, 2021: a grand jury returned a seventeen-count D.C. indictment. The indictment remains a charging instrument rather than the final disposition.
- January 12, 2021: Magistrate Judge G. Michael Harvey ordered pretrial detention.
- May 24, 2021: Judge Colleen Kollar-Kotelly denied Coffman's motion to revoke the detention order in ECF No. 19. The opinion addressed Bail Reform Act standards, not final guilt.
- November 12, 2021: Coffman pleaded guilty to two federal counts under 26 U.S.C. § 5861(d), one arising in D.C. and one transferred from Alabama, and one D.C. pistol-license count.
- April 1, 2022: Judge Kollar-Kotelly imposed 46 months on each federal count and 15 months on the D.C. count, all concurrent, followed by three years of supervised release.
- January 20, 2025: the general full-pardon provision of the January 6 clemency proclamation applied to covered convicted defendants outside the named commutation group. The Office of the Pardon Attorney separately lists Coffman as a pardon-certificate recipient.
