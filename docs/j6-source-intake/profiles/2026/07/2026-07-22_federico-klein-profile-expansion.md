## Verdict

On July 20, 2023, the Justice Department reported that Judge McFadden found Klein guilty after a bench trial of:

- Six felony counts of assaulting, resisting, or impeding certain officers and aiding and abetting
- Obstruction of an official proceeding and aiding and abetting
- Civil disorder
- Misdemeanor disorderly conduct in a Capitol building
- Misdemeanor act of physical violence in the Capitol grounds or building

Descriptions of Klein's conduct in the DOJ releases are government and court-record descriptions and must remain attributed as such.

## Sentence

On November 3, 2023, Judge McFadden sentenced Klein to:

- 70 months of imprisonment
- 24 months of supervised release
- $5,000 in combined fines and restitution

## Clemency

President Donald J. Trump's January 20, 2025 proclamation commuted the sentences of 14 specifically named people and granted a full, complete, and unconditional pardon to all other individuals convicted of covered January 6 offenses. Klein was not one of the 14 named commutation recipients; his covered convictions therefore fell within the proclamation's pardon provision.

This source bundle does not claim that the pardon erased the historical judgment or that a court separately vacated the conviction. A native post-pardon docket entry, judgment notation, or certificate for Klein has not yet been preserved.
