# Joseph Hackett and Oath Keepers case-record expansion

## Purpose

This bundle corrects Joseph Hackett's profile, which previously treated the January 23, 2023 jury verdict as a sentencing event and described the January 20, 2025 clemency action generically. The corrected record separates verdict, sentence commutation, appellate vacatur, remand, and the still-pending district-court dismissal request.

## Verified case chronology

- January 23, 2023: A jury found Joseph Hackett, Roberto Minuta, David Moerschel, and Edward Vallejo guilty of seditious conspiracy, conspiracy to obstruct an official proceeding, obstruction of an official proceeding, and conspiracy to prevent Members of Congress from discharging their duties. Hackett was also found guilty of destruction of evidence.
- June 2, 2023: Judge Amit P. Mehta sentenced Hackett to 42 months' imprisonment and 36 months of supervised release.
- January 20, 2025: President Donald J. Trump commuted Hackett's sentence to time served. The proclamation listed Hackett among the fourteen commutation recipients; it did not classify that relief as a full pardon.
- May 21, 2026: Published reporting and the NACDL case-file index indicate that the D.C. Circuit vacated the affected Oath Keepers judgments and remanded for consideration of a government dismissal motion.
- May 22, 2026: The government filed ECF No. 967, an unopposed motion to dismiss the indictment with prejudice.
- May 29, 2026: Judge Mehta entered ECF No. 976 regarding the unopposed dismissal motion and deferred final disposition while requiring a fuller explanation.
- Through this July 22, 2026 review, no later final district-court dismissal order for the Oath Keepers case was verified.
