## Rosanne Boyland claim — verification boundary

The defense sentencing memorandum states that Stager tried to aid injured people at the Lower West Terrace, including an unidentified apparently lifeless person whom he helped move toward aid. The memorandum does **not** identify that person as Rosanne Boyland. Geographic and temporal overlap alone is insufficient to establish identity.

The archive therefore records the defense account accurately but does not state as fact that Stager helped Boyland. Resolution requires a verified time-synchronized video record, an authenticated firsthand identification, or another source expressly identifying the person.
