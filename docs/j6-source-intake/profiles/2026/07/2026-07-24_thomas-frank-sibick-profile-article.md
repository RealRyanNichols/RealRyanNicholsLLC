- The detention order and D.C. Circuit judgment are procedural risk rulings, not final findings of guilt.
- The broad indictment is distinguished from the three offenses of conviction.
- The plea, sentence and pardon are procedural facts, not judgments about character.
- The pardon is distinguished from acquittal, vacatur or appellate reversal.
