## Source-resolution notes

- The January 20, 2025 proclamation names Stewart Rhodes in the commutation list. It does not grant him a full pardon.
- The DOJ release identifies May 25, 2023 as the sentence date. The prior profile's May 23 date is corrected.
- DOJ states that Rhodes remained outside the Capitol building.
- The quick-reaction-force weapons allegation is attributed to prosecutors. Reporting reviewed for this intake states the staged weapons were not deployed.
- The terrorism language belongs to the sentencing-guideline enhancement and court finding; it is not a separate count of conviction.
- The April 14, 2026 government request is a motion, not a final vacatur. No later dispositive Oath Keepers order was verified in the reviewed public source set through July 22.
- The Proud Boys July 2026 vacatur and dismissal must not be copied onto Rhodes' separate case without a Rhodes-specific order.
