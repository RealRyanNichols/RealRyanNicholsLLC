## Verified procedural summary

Larocca was arrested March 26, 2021 and indicted April 23, 2021 on five counts. He pleaded guilty April 8, 2022 to Count Three, disorderly and disruptive conduct in a restricted building or grounds under 18 U.S.C. § 1752(a)(2). Judge Tanya S. Chutkan sentenced him August 10, 2022 to 60 days' imprisonment, 12 months' supervised release, 60 hours of community service, a $2,000 fine, $500 restitution, and a $25 special assessment. The judgment dismissed Counts One, Two, Four, and Five. Because Larocca was not one of the proclamation's fourteen named commutation recipients, his covered conviction falls within the full-pardon provision.
