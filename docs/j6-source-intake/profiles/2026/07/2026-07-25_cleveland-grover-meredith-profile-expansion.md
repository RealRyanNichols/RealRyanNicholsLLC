## Verified procedural record

- January 6, 2021: Meredith arrived in Washington after the Capitol events had ended, according to the signed statement of offense.
- January 7, 2021: FBI contact and arrest at a Washington hotel, according to DOJ and the plea record.
- February 26, 2021: federal indictment.
- April 2, 2021: four-count superseding indictment.
- May 26, 2021: detention opinion recorded on the public docket; native opinion not captured.
- September 10, 2021: guilty plea to Count One, interstate communication of threats under 18 U.S.C. § 875(c), pursuant to a written Rule 11(c)(1)(B) agreement.
- December 14, 2021: 28 months' imprisonment with credit for time served, 36 months' supervised release, and a $100 assessment.
- December 21, 2021: signed judgment filed; all remaining counts dismissed on the government's motion.
- January 23, 2025: attributed public statement reporting that federal probation told Meredith he was fully pardoned.

The preserved case materials do not establish that Meredith entered the Capitol. The statement of offense says he arrived after the Capitol events had ended.
