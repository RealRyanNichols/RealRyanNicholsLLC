- Corrected the profile from a generic charge list to the actual Eighth Superseding Indictment verdict outcomes.
- Recorded guilty verdicts on Count 1, conspiracy to obstruct an official proceeding under 18 U.S.C. § 1512(k), and Count 5, entering or remaining in restricted building or grounds under 18 U.S.C. § 1752(a)(1).

- Added Judge Amit P. Mehta and defense counsel Stephen F. Brennwald.
