## 79 Minutes source boundary

The verified Discerning History page describes the film as following the period from the initial Capitol-grounds breach to the building breach and examining multiple individuals, groups, police preparation, police tactics, crowd behavior, and the failure of the building defense. The official project site says the documentary used reports, eyewitness interviews, and nearly 200 synchronized video clips.

These statements establish what the publishers say the film covers. They do not independently verify every narrative or causal conclusion in the film. Person-specific claims must be connected only after clip-level review.

The publisher page is dated January 2, 2024. Other public indexes display a January 3 release date or an earlier September 2023 availability announcement. The archive uses the verified publisher-page date for the document record and retains the date discrepancy as a research note rather than silently resolving it.
