## Verified holding and boundaries

The court granted summary judgment to Officers Kevin Peralta and Aaron Smith on Blair's federal claims because they were entitled to qualified immunity. The court denied Blair's cross-motion for summary judgment and remanded his remaining D.C.-law assault-and-battery and negligent-training/supervision claims to D.C. Superior Court.

The opinion states that, when the evidence is viewed in Blair's favor as required at summary judgment, a reasonable jury could find the baton strikes objectively unreasonable. It also recounts that an MPD internal investigation found the challenged force unjustified under department policy. The federal claims nevertheless failed because Blair did not identify sufficiently similar precedent clearly establishing a constitutional violation in the specific circumstances.

This ruling is not a final finding that the officers are civilly liable, does not resolve the remanded D.C.-law claims, and does not convert allegations or advocacy into established facts. The archive must preserve the distinction among the summary-judgment standard, the qualified-immunity holding, the internal-investigation finding quoted by the court, and any future state-court disposition.
