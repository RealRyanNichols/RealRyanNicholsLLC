## Source summary

The U.S. Department of Justice Office of Information Policy released a seven-page redacted packet titled **“Call Logs for Senior Officials from January 6, 2021.”** The packet contains January 6 call-log records associated with Acting Attorney General Jeffrey A. Rosen, Acting Deputy Attorney General Richard P. Donoghue, Office of the Deputy Attorney General official John P. Moran, and additional handwritten or typed senior-official call lists.

The released sheets record times, durations, origination and destination fields, redactions, and OIP-supplied labels identifying some telephone numbers. The record does not contain call audio, transcripts, descriptions of what was discussed, or independent proof of the purpose of any call.

## Attribution and verification boundary

OIP states that, because of circumstances unique to the request, it had access to additional information used to place some caller identities inside redaction boxes. OIP further states that the information is not ordinarily present in call logs and that OIP could not independently verify the accuracy of the identities associated with those phone numbers.

Accordingly:

- Caller names are preserved as **OIP annotations**, not independently authenticated identity findings.
- A call entry is not treated as proof of agreement, misconduct, intent, or any particular statement.
- The source is not connected to individual January 6 defendants without person-specific evidence.
- Editorial analysis concerning government conduct remains separate from the underlying technical record.
