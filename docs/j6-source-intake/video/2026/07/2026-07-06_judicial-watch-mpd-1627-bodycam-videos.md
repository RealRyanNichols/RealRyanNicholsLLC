# MPD January 6 body-camera corpus: 1,627 released videos

## Verified source summary

Judicial Watch reports that the Metropolitan Police Department of the District of Columbia released **1,627 body-worn-camera videos totaling more than 1,000 hours** concerning the police response at and around the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021.

The searchable collection page is dated July 6, 2026. Judicial Watch separately highlighted the completed production on July 10, 2026.

## Court provenance

The production followed litigation captioned **Judicial Watch v. District of Columbia, No. 2024-CAB-003453**, in the Superior Court of the District of Columbia.

Judicial Watch states that it requested all MPD body-camera audio and video recorded by officers responding to January 6 activity, including all body-camera footage captured by Officer Michael Fanone. The organization filed suit after MPD denied the request.

Judicial Watch's court-ruling summary reports that the court rejected blanket redaction of non-law-enforcement faces and voices. According to that summary and the quoted order language, the court concluded that the public interest in disclosure outweighed the comparatively limited privacy interests asserted for people recorded in public during the event.
