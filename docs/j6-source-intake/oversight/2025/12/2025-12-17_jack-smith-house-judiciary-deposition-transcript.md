## Why this source matters

The official 255-page redacted transcript records former Special Counsel Jack Smith's December 17, 2025 deposition to the U.S. House Committee on the Judiciary. It supplies the complete context for excerpts later cited in the Committee chairman's July 22, 2026 referral to the Justice Department.

The questioning addresses:

- Executive-privilege disputes.

## Toll-record testimony and later dispute

Smith described telephone toll records as non-content records containing information such as the originating and receiving numbers, call timing, and duration. He testified that the toll-record subpoenas discussed did not contain the content of calls or text messages.

The July 22, 2026 House Judiciary referral argues that some answers were materially incomplete when compared with a separately disclosed National Archives production of 54 spreadsheets containing White House-phone text messages. Smith's attorneys dispute that characterization and distinguish the non-content toll-record subpoenas from the National Archives production.

The archive preserves the transcript, the later referral, the DOJ filter-team letter, and the underlying filter-review exhibit. It does not treat the referral as a charge, prosecution, conviction, or judicial finding.
