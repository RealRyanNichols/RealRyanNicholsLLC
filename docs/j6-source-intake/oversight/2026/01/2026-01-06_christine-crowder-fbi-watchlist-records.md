## Why this source matters

The U.S. Senate Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs and Chairman Rand Paul released a 70-page packet containing a committee flash report, a detailed timeline, and redacted FBI records concerning the 23-month investigation of **Christine Crowder**, a Texas Catholic school teacher who attended President Donald J. Trump's Ellipse rally on January 6, 2021.

The committee materials state that the FBI opened the investigation after an unverified tip from a former friend, received negative initial geolocation and facial-recognition results, later recommended Crowder for watchlisting and Expanded Selectee treatment, conducted physical surveillance, and referred the matter for prosecution before positively identifying her. The released timeline states that the FBI closed the investigation on June 6, 2023 after a rideshare receipt, clothing evidence, negative searches, and a confidential-human-source photograph supported mistaken identity.

No captured source identifies a criminal charge, plea, conviction, or sentence against Crowder.

## Verified timeline from the released packet

- **January 11, 2021:** FBI receives the tip.
- **June 25, 2021:** FBI opens a full investigation despite reported negative geolocation and facial-recognition searches.
- **July 2, 2021:** FBI conducts physical surveillance of the Crowder household.
- **July 14, 2021:** FBI paperwork recommends adding Crowder to the Terrorist Screening Dataset.
- **August 2, 2021:** FBI travel notification records watchlisting and recommended Expanded Selectee treatment.
- **February 13 and April 26, 2022:** additional physical surveillance is documented.
- **February 23, 2022:** FBI receives a video from the tipster showing a person the tipster believed was Crowder.
- **May 24, 2022:** FBI interviews the original tipster and spouse.
- **July 7, 2022:** released records state the U.S. Attorney's Office accepted the matter for prosecution despite the FBI's inability to positively identify Crowder.
- **July 20, 2022:** further physical surveillance is documented.
- **October 27, 2022:** FBI executes a Facebook search warrant through Facebook's law-enforcement portal.
- **May 2, 2023:** FBI interviews Crowder; the released timeline says she produced clothing and a rideshare receipt inconsistent with the allegation that she was inside the Capitol.
- **May 4, 2023:** a confidential human source provides a photograph showing Crowder's January 6 attire.
- **June 6, 2023:** FBI closes the investigation; the released timeline cites negative geofence and facial-recognition results, the rideshare receipt, and clothing inconsistent with the original tip.
- **September 30, 2025:** Mark Crowder testifies before HSGAC concerning the Quiet Skies program and surveillance of his family.
- **January 6, 2026:** Chairman Paul publishes the additional FBI records and committee findings.
