## March 13, 2026 civil opinion

White's amended civil complaint alleged that two Metropolitan Police Department officers used excessive force against her in the Lower West Terrace tunnel. The opinion summarizes allegations of repeated baton strikes, punches, crowd crush, being pushed against a concrete wall, and an approximately thirty-second interruption in one officer's body-camera recording.

Those descriptions remain allegations attributed to White's pleading. The court did not independently establish every alleged act as fact.

Judge Carl J. Nichols granted the officers judgment on the pleadings on qualified-immunity grounds. The opinion assumed for purposes of the analysis that a constitutional violation may have occurred, but held that White had not shown that the asserted right was clearly established in the particular tunnel circumstances. The court therefore did not need to definitively decide whether the alleged force constituted a constitutional violation.

The opinion described White's alleged treatment as harrowing while concluding that qualified immunity controlled the legal disposition. Both parts of that record are preserved.
