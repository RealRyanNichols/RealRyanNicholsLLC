## Verified procedural record

On March 30, 2026, the district court denied Brian Mock's and Cindy Lou Young's motions to intervene in litigation brought by Harry Dunn and Daniel Hodges concerning the congressionally directed January 6 law-enforcement plaque.

The opinion held that Mock and Young had not alleged a concrete and particularized injury sufficient for Article III standing to seek relief broader than the original plaintiffs requested. The court also declined permissive intervention because the proposed relief—a materially different or broader memorial—did not share the required legal or factual basis with the plaintiffs' statutory plaque claim and could delay or prejudice the original action.

The ruling addressed standing and intervention. It did **not** adjudicate whether every factual or political assertion made by Mock or Young about January 6, detention, treatment, stigma, public history, or government conduct was true or false.
