## Verified holding

The court unanimously affirmed the district court judgment against Peter K. Navarro on two counts of contempt of Congress arising from his noncompliance with a subpoena issued by the House Select Committee investigating January 6.

The opinion held that the authority to invoke executive privilege belongs to a president or an authorized designee. The panel concluded that the district court did not clearly err in finding that neither President Donald Trump nor a designee invoked executive privilege for the subpoena at issue. The court also concluded that Navarro's categorical noncompliance included material that could not have been protected by executive privilege.
