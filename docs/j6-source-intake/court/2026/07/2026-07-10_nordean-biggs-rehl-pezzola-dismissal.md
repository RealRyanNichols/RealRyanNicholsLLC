# United States v. Nordean et al. — dismissal with prejudice

## Verified procedural update

On July 10, 2026, U.S. District Judge Timothy J. Kelly granted the federal government's unopposed Rule 48(a) motion and dismissed the case with prejudice against Ethan Nordean, Joseph Biggs, Zachary Rehl, and Dominic Pezzola.

The memorandum states that the D.C. Circuit had already vacated the four judgments on May 21, 2026 after the government requested vacatur and remand. The July 10 dismissal permanently ended the indictment against these four defendants in Criminal Action No. 21-175.
