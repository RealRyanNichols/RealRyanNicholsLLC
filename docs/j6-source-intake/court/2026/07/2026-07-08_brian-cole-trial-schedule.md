## Verified procedural update

Multiple independent reports state that Judge Ali scheduled Cole's trial to begin February 16, 2027. ABC News and Associated Press reporting identify February 16 as the start date and estimate a trial length of approximately two weeks. ABC also reports that the next status conference is set for September 10, 2026. The Washington Examiner expressly identifies the year as 2027, resolving the abbreviated “Feb. 16” wording used in the ABC and AP reports.

Cole has pleaded not guilty. The scheduling decision does not establish the truth of the government's allegations, guilt, motive, or moral character.
