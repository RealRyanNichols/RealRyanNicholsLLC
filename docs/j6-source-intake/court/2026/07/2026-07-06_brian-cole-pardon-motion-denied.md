# United States v. Brian J. Cole, Jr. — July 6, 2026 pardon-scope ruling

## Court result

On July 6, 2026, U.S. District Judge Amir H. Ali denied Brian J. Cole, Jr.'s motion to dismiss the pending indictment under President Donald J. Trump's January 20, 2025 clemency proclamation.

The court's stated reasons were procedural and textual:

- The proclamation's pardon language applied to people already convicted of covered offenses; Cole had not been convicted.
- The separate directive concerning dismissal of pending indictments did not apply because Cole was first charged in December 2025, after the January 20, 2025 directive.
- The denial did not adjudicate whether Cole committed the charged conduct. The criminal charges remain allegations, and the case remains pending.
