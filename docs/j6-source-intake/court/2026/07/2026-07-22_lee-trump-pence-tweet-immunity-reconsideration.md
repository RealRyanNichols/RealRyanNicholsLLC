## Material development

On July 22, 2026, Judge Amit P. Mehta declined to reconsider his earlier conclusion that Donald J. Trump’s January 6, 2021 2:24 p.m. tweet concerning Vice President Mike Pence could be treated as an official presidential act for civil-immunity purposes in the consolidated civil litigation led by *Lee v. Trump*, No. `1:21-cv-00400-APM`.

The court nevertheless certified the narrow immunity question for interlocutory appellate review. That procedural step permits the plaintiffs to seek immediate review in the U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit before final judgment in the consolidated cases.

Contemporaneous reporting quotes the court as stating that the tweet “arguably poured gasoline on the fire,” while also reasoning that a presidential response to an attack on a coordinate branch could qualify as an official act. The archive preserves both parts of the court’s analysis rather than using either phrase as a standalone characterization.
