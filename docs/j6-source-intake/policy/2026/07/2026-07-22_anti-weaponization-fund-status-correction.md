## Meaningful update

The Justice Department publicly announced the Anti-Weaponization Fund on May 18, 2026. The announcement described a planned $1.776 billion process for claims alleging political or ideological government weaponization.

By June 5, 2026, however, Justice Department lawyers told two federal courts that the fund had not been established and was “not going forward.” Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche also stated publicly that DOJ was not moving forward with the fund. Later litigation continued over whether that position was sufficiently formal and binding.

A third-party page updated July 22, 2026 presented the fund as an active compensation program for January 6 defendants, supplied a December 2028 deadline, and described categories of recoverable damages. No current official application portal, active claims process, or verified deadline supporting that presentation was found during this review.

The archive therefore must not describe the fund as currently open or state that a January 6 claim is being submitted to it. The accurate public posture is that DOJ announced the fund and later represented in court that it had not been set up and was not proceeding, while related litigation and requests for formal assurances continued.
