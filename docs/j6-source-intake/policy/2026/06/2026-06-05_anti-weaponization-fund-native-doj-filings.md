## Material correction

The Justice Department announced a proposed Anti-Weaponization Fund on May 18, 2026. On June 5, 2026, DOJ filed parallel court responses stating that the fund had not been set up and was not going forward. Both filings said no money had been transferred, no formal claims mechanism existed, and none of the contemplated fund members had been appointed.

These filings document the government's litigation position. They are not treated as final merits judgments resolving every claim in the two civil cases.

A third-party page updated July 22, 2026 continued to state that January 6 defendants could apply, supplied a December 2028 deadline, estimated award ranges, and said an official portal would open. That page is preserved as a contradicted public source and not as official application guidance.
