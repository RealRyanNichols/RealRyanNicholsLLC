## Verified summary

The Office of the Pardon Attorney states that it is ready to issue certificates of pardon to people covered by President Donald J. Trump’s January 20, 2025 January 6 clemency proclamation.

The official instructions direct a requester to email `USPardon.Attorney@usdoj.gov` with the subject line **“January 20, 2025 Pardon Certificate”** and include:

1. Full name;
2. Prison register number, if any; and
3. District Court case number.

The office states that it will email a copy of the certificate and asks requesters to allow at least ten business days for processing.

## Editorial and verification boundaries

- These instructions are an official administrative route for requesting a certificate; they do not independently establish that every person who submits an email is covered by the proclamation.
- A person-specific archive record should identify a certificate as received only after the certificate itself, an official recipient listing, or another reliable primary source is captured.
- The page concerns certificates of **pardon**. The January 20 proclamation separately commuted the sentences of fourteen named people; the archive should not describe a commutation recipient as pardoned without a separate, source-supported pardon.
- Do not publish personal prison-register numbers or private correspondence without a lawful archival reason and appropriate privacy review.
