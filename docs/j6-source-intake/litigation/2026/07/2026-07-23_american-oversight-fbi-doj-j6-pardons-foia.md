## Verified summary

American Oversight filed a Freedom of Information Act action against the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the U.S. Department of Justice seeking senior-official communications concerning January 6, election certification, and the January 20, 2025 pardons and commutations. The public docket identifies the case as `1:26-cv-00020`, assigned to Judge Ana C. Reyes, and shows that it remains ongoing.

The complaint is publicly listed by DocumentCloud as a 55-page filing. The plaintiff describes five FOIA requests seeking emails and text messages from senior DOJ and FBI officials, including communications concerning January 6 and clemency. Those descriptions are the plaintiff's characterization of the requested records and the alleged failure to produce them; they are not findings that any official engaged in misconduct.

The docket shows status reports filed on April 14, April 28, and June 29, 2026. The April 28 public summary states that agency searches were ongoing and that the parties proposed another report by June 29. The June 29 entry is verified as existing, but its native contents were not available through the current capture path. Notices of appearance and withdrawal were entered on July 14 and July 15.
