# Tarrio v. United States — July 2026 docket status

## What was preserved

The public RECAP/Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse docket for *Tarrio v. United States of America*, No. 6:25-cv-00998, was reviewed and preserved for the January 6 archive.

The plaintiffs are Enrique Tarrio, Joseph Biggs, Ethan Nordean, Dominic Pezzola, and Zachary Rehl. Their civil complaint alleges constitutional violations and malicious prosecution arising from the federal January 6 prosecutions. Those allegations remain the plaintiffs' claims and have not been adjudicated as established facts.

- April 10, 2026: Judge Anne-Leigh Gaylord Moe dismissed the original complaint without prejudice and granted leave to amend. The defendants' then-pending dismissal motions were denied as moot without prejudice.
