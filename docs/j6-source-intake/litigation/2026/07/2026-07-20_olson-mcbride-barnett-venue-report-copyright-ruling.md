## Verified procedural record

On July 20, 2026, Judge Naomi Reice Buchwald denied Joseph D. McBride and McBride Law Firm, PLLC’s motions to dismiss Lindsay Olson’s copyright complaint.

The opinion recounts allegations that Olson prepared a report titled “MultiDistrict Comparative Community Attitude Study” for use in venue-transfer motions by two attorneys representing defendants in the Oath Keepers prosecution. It further recounts Olson’s allegation that McBride and his firm obtained the report and filed it in September 2022 in *United States v. Barnett*, D.D.C. No. 1:21-cr-00038, as an exhibit supporting Richard Barnett’s venue-transfer motion.

At the Rule 12(b)(6) stage, the court declined to dismiss on the asserted grounds that:

- filing through ECF did not implicate rights protected by the Copyright Act;
- the filing was necessarily fair use as a matter of law;
- public-access, First Amendment, due-process, or effective-assistance principles categorically foreclosed liability;
- the claim was clearly time-barred; or
- the complaint failed to plausibly allege available actual damages.

The court terminated the motions pending at ECF Nos. 16 and 17 and encouraged the parties to consider resolution rather than additional litigation expense.

## Verification boundary

This is a pleading-stage ruling, not a final merits judgment. It allowed Olson’s claim to proceed but did not establish copyright infringement, damages, bad faith, or the ultimate fair-use result.

Richard Barnett is not a party to the copyright action. The opinion expressly characterizes the report as a commercially licensed advocacy tool rather than evidence of Barnett’s guilt or innocence. The archive connects the ruling to Barnett because it verifies the report’s filing in his criminal docket and later judicial discussion of that filing.

The opinion’s background section summarizes complaint allegations under the motion-to-dismiss standard. Those allegations are not restated as independently established facts beyond what the filing itself verifies.
